## What was changed

Only the formatting. The text was taken from the Library's PDFs; lines broken mid-sentence were
joined back into the original paragraphs, and speaker labels were made consistent ("Library of
Congress:" for "LOC", "LC" and "LIBRARY OF CONGRESS"; the singer's full name for "DP", "CL",
"AK", "AC" and "CYNDI LAUPER"). The words, including the editors' notes in [square brackets],
are unchanged. Each file starts with a short note giving the source and permission.

More interviews, as PDFs: https://www.loc.gov/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/recording-registry/index-of-interviews/
